## H1 — Remote bro pair: deep work + lift + breath inside one persistent 1:1 protocol — killed 2026-04-26
- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 0 / 27 (across 10 findings); 1.5 / 27 if scoped to the 4 separation-frame quotes
- Cause of death: WTP — zero quotes matched H1's specific "buy this for me and my bro" / Focusmate-churn signal; matchmaking pain dominated 5:1 over continuity pain (H1's own named kill condition); zero quotes stacked body + work + breath in a single user voice.
- Lesson: A wedge that is true in our personal life but doesn't show up as a verbatim complaint anywhere on the internet across 40+ targeted searches is a thesis, not a market — when matchmaking-pain volume drowns continuity-pain 5:1, you're staring at a different product than the one you wanted to build.

## H4 — Weird-men's-depth cohort: paid 8-week tribe for high-agency men who want body + mind + brotherhood with receipts — killed 2026-04-26
- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 2 / 27 (across 15 findings); top score 12 (F8) is structurally wrong-audience
- Cause of death: WTP signal exists at the right price band ($500-$20K) but lives entirely inside cult-framed competitors (Sacred Sons, MDKP, UPW) whose buyer base is "MLM types / motivational addicts" (F9), not H4's high-agency Koe/Naval/Huberman tribe; the closest churn quote (F13) shows a man LEAVING a paid container to start a free one (anti-WTP); zero verbatim matches for "I'd pay $500-1500 for an 8-week body+mind+brotherhood cohort"; F1/F2 demonstrate hard stated-preference vs revealed-behavior gap (men flake on free versions); founder-fit kill fired per H4's own spec — community-as-product is cohort facilitation operations, not software, and Efe+Bingen are software founders.
- Lesson: When the WTP price band is real but every existing buyer is in a cult-suspect org and the public default response to the price is "go to therapy," the category is structurally exiting — buyers walk away from it, not toward a better version. Also: the founder-fit kill axis is load-bearing — even hypothetical strong signal would not flip this for software founders, because cohort businesses are operational businesses. Don't bother validating community-as-product hypotheses unless one of the founders actively wants to run cohorts.

- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 0 / 27 (across 11 scored findings; F12 logged as competitor evidence, not user-pain)
- Cause of death: WTP — none of H3's four named WTP phrases ("I have 5 apps and use none" / "wish Whoop talked to my calendar" / ex-Future churn / "$20-50/mo for AI coach") appeared verbatim in 12 HN findings (Reddit blocked); 6 of 11 scored findings hit 0 on WTP. The strong $ signals that did surface (F1: $65/mo coach.me × 4yr "still in the same spot"; F8: "few hundred dollars a month" for trainer "the last thing I want to do is put together a workout plan") map to the human-accountability-coach-replacement archetype, NOT to the wearable+cowork+breath three-stack the hypothesis required. Zero quotes contained the integrated three-stack framing in user voice (per hunter's "did NOT find" log). - Lesson: Founder-flavored "ONE app to run my whole day" framings live on the builder side of the table, not the user side — when 12 findings can name execution-vs-knowledge pain (F2, F11) and dollar-on-the-table accountability-coach pain (F1, F8) but NONE of them describe stacking lift + work + meditation as a single product, that stacking is our thesis, not their language. The salvageable shape is "AI replacement for the $200/mo human accountability coach" (F1+F8 line up clean) — that's a different hypothesis, not a re-hunt of H3. Also: when the hypothesis names specific WTP phrases and the hunt cannot surface them, kill regardless of high intensity elsewhere — "don't move the goalposts."

## H5 — AI replacement for the $65–$300/mo human accountability coach who isn't actually pushing you — killed 2026-04-26
- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 0 / 27 (across 15 findings; mean 0.73)
- Cause of death: STRUCTURAL — three of H5's six self-set kill criteria fired independently. (1) Kill #1 (paying-customer churn quotes): only F1 (nebula8804, coach.me $64.99/mo × 4 yrs "still in the same spot") cleanly matched H5 phrase-shape #1; threshold was 5, found 1; phrase-shapes #2 (human coach not pushing hard enough) and #3 (ChatGPT-as-coach + memory failure tied to coaching) returned zero verbatim hits per the hunter's own "did NOT find" log. (2) Kill #4 (competitor saturation): 30+ shipped AI-coach products with confirmed-alive homepages — coach.me 5,000+ customers, AthleteData ~50→500 paying in <1mo at $9/mo, Shimmer $172-230/mo with named outcomes, plus 23+ vertical Show HNs since 2023 with cadence accelerating to >1/mo. (3) Kill #5 (Bingen-fit absent): zero findings surface a "and the meditation/breath layer is the differentiator" angle — F1's pain is depth-of-push, F12's is sycophancy, F13/F14's is LLM memory; none plug into Eclipta IP. Counter-evidence (F4 $10k+ satisfied, F5 insanely-expensive-but-ok, F6 $50/mo neutral) outweighs churn 3:1 in HN data — the "everyone is unhappy with their human coach" narrative isn't what the data shows.
- Lesson: When the hunter's own report flags structural kill conditions BEFORE pain-validator runs, the validator's job is to honor the hypothesis's self-set kill criteria, not to find a way to keep the hypothesis alive on intensity grounds. Pain density and WTP can be high (F1 = $3,100 spent, F4 = $10k+ spent) and the wedge can still be dead because the market is saturated with shipped competitors AND the founder-fit lane has no Eclipta plug-in. Structural kills are independent of pain density — "we'd compete on quality" is not an escape that the hypothesis's own spec allows. The salvageable signal here is the LLM-memory-ceiling pattern (F11 "more push back," F13 "constantly need to remind it," F14 "forgets things committed to memory," F15 builder-customer with private GitHub context) — but that's H7's territory (BYOC persistent-context wrapper for technical DIY users), not a re-hunt of H5. Move energy to H7.

## H2 — Phone-rage reset: anti-distraction blocker + 60-second breath grounding in one loop — killed 2026-04-26 (REDUNDANT — Bingen already shipped it)
- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 0/27 (verdict was MAYBE not GO at validation; HN intensity strong, Reddit blocked, EvoCat flagged as competitor risk)
- Cause of death: REDUNDANT, not market-failed. Discovered after the full agent chain ran: Bingen's Eclipta (App Store ID 6757725265, "Eclipta: Screen Time & Focus", launched 2026-04-07, version 1.0.5, ₺499/mo) is *already* a block+meditate combo. The Goals Tab feature ships exactly H2's wedge: "distracting apps are blocked until the user meditates." The framing "merge Efe's Blocker IP with Bingen's Eclipta meditation IP" was wrong on premise — Eclipta IS the merge, three weeks old at time of writing. The H2 evidence (15 HN findings, ScreenZen Discord, Opal community forum, Cal Newport audience) doesn't disappear — it now justifies Eclipta's existence, not a new product.
- Lesson: **Read the actual product before treating its IP as a load-bearing assumption.** I assumed "Eclipta = meditation app" from Efe's life-os notes (which said "meditation app on the App Store") and never opened the App Store listing. That assumption persisted through 7 hypotheses, 4 hunts, 4 validators, a community map, and a build-in-public draft set. The cost was building a "merge" thesis (H2) for a product Bingen already shipped, plus polluting H3/H5/H7 graveyard lessons with "no Eclipta IP fit" framing that was based on a wrong picture of what Eclipta IP actually is. Next time: when a cofounding analysis hangs on the other founder's IP, the first 60 seconds of research is reading their App Store / website / GitHub readme verbatim, not paraphrasing from second-hand notes. Also: this collision means Efe's solo Blocker product is in the same lane as Bingen's already-shipped Eclipta — a decision Efe must make on the call (kill Blocker, differentiate it hard, or absorb it into Eclipta). See `notes/call-prep-bingen.md` for revised options.

## H7 — Bring-your-own-context persistent AI coach for ChatGPT power users hitting memory/continuity walls — killed 2026-04-26
- Hypothesized: 2026-04-26
- Median pain score: 0 / 27 (across 10 scored findings; six zeros driven by WTP=0, three findings hit 4, F7 hits 0 on every axis)
- Cause of death: STRUCTURAL — three independent kill axes all fired, any one sufficient. (1) Platform risk (kill criterion #2 verbatim): OpenAI shipped "memory references all chats" April 2025, Anthropic shipped Claude Memory to Pro/Max consumer plans October 2025, Microsoft Copilot Memory 2026-Q1 — the platform ship that H7 named as a kill condition already happened 12 months ago, and F5 ("My OpenAI ChatGPT knows me VERY well") + F7 ("glimpse of something big") prove the platform fix is "good enough" for at least some H7-persona users. Sam Altman publicly states "infinite, perfect memory" is OpenAI's competitive strategy — they are actively eating this wedge. (2) Builder/buyer asymmetry inverts the thesis: F4 evoke4908 ("please for the love of god do not use someone else's AI for this... A damn toaster can run a small ollama") = high-intensity NEGATIVE WTP from the persona-perfect technical user; F2 gchallen describes Claude Coach as "fun... in no rush" with no buyer-WTP attached; F1, F3, F8 are all founders pitching their own H7-shape products on HN, not buyers. Every persona match in the hunt who articulated H7's pain BUILT it themselves. Zero "I'd pay to skip building" quotes. (3) Lane saturation (kill criterion #4 fired multiple times over): 15+ Show HN persistent-memory products in 6 months (Eve, IronClaude, Bob, Bossa, Bind.ly, Matrix OS, PearlOS, Athena), tier-1 funded incumbents in every adjacent cell (Rosebud $6M Bessemer, Cleo $250M ARR / 1M paid subs, Replika 30M downloads, Character.AI), commodity infra at $0 npm (Mem0, Letta, Supermemory). Pi.ai/Inflection imploded at $1.5B as cautionary tale for "personal AI" thesis. Bingen-fit fail (kill criterion #5): memory/context-engineering has no plug-in for Eclipta IP. WTP signal absent across all three named phrase-shapes: F9 RickS ("$20 easy... $200/mo reasonable") is the strongest single quote and it's a hypothetical wishlist for a generic second-brain, not paying-customer churn for an AI coach above ChatGPT Plus — capped at WTP=2 per validator instruction.
- Lesson: When the hunt's own kill criterion names platform risk and the platforms (OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft) have publicly committed to BEING the wedge ("infinite, perfect memory" — Altman; "ai systems that get to know you over your life"), the wedge is structurally over before the build starts — wrappers on frontier-LLM platform layers get steamrolled by the next ship cycle, and the lock-in is on the platform, not on us (F10 thread proves this: when OpenAI broke memory, paying users filed bugs and WAITED for OpenAI rather than switching to a wrapper). Also: the builder-vs-buyer asymmetry is now a load-bearing pattern across H1/H3/H7 — when every persona match in the hunt BUILDS the product themselves and posts to HN about how fun it was, the buyers don't exist; technical-enough-to-build does NOT mean technical-enough-to-pay-to-skip-building (F4 proves it usually means the OPPOSITE — they actively recommend others self-host for free). Don't validate hypotheses where the canonical user is also the canonical builder unless one of them has a churn-from-DIY quote with $ attached — wishlist comments don't count. Stop respawning AI-coach hypotheses (H3, H5, H7 all dead in one day): the pattern across them is consistent — saturated lane, no Bingen-fit, builder/buyer asymmetry, no Eclipta plug-in. Zoom out before generating H8.
