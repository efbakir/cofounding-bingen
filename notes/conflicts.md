# Conflicts — 24h cooling area

Points where the two of us disagree. Write it down, wait 24 hours, then talk. Once resolved, move it to `notes/decisions.md`.

## Format

```
## {Topic} — opened YYYY-MM-DD

**Efe says**: {position, 2-3 sentences}
**Bingen says**: {position, 2-3 sentences}
**Crux**: {where the real disagreement lies — values, data, risk perception?}
**Resolve target**: YYYY-MM-DD (24h after opening, minimum)
```
