## The two corrections you should know going in

### 1. The original wedge didn't survive market validation
**"Remote parallel-build app — deep work + lift + meditation alongside one chosen bro. Like Focusmate but for the body."** — KILLED. Median pain score 0/27 across 10 findings. r/Focusmate is dead (135 members). 1 verbatim quote out of 10 had separation+WTP, gym-only — no work/breath stack. Matchmaking pain dominated continuity pain **5:1** (the hypothesis's own kill condition).

Lived experience didn't translate to a paying complaint anywhere on the public internet. **The story you and Bingen lived in Milan is real. The market wanting it as software is not.**

### 2. Eclipta isn't a meditation app — it's already a block+meditate combo
The original analysis treated Eclipta as a pure meditation app and framed H2 as "merge Efe's Blocker + Bingen's meditation engine." Wrong premise.

**What Eclipta actually is** (verified from https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/eclipta-screen-time-focus/id6757725265):
- Name: **"Eclipta: Screen Time & Focus"**
- Tagline: *"Master your focus by bridging the gap between meditation and digital wellness."*
- **Goals Tab feature: "distracting apps are blocked until the user meditates."** ← this IS what H2 was framed as merging.
- Pricing: ₺499.99/mo or ₺3,999.99/yr Pro tier (~$14 / ~$115)
- Released: **2026-04-07** (3 weeks old at time of writing)
- Version 1.0.5, no ratings yet
- Devices: iOS 18.6+, iPadOS, macOS (M1+), visionOS — Apple-only, no Android
- Other features: Meditation Hub timer, Digital Wellness Analytics (DeviceActivity framework), leveling Novice→Enlightened, monthly streaks calendar, 35+ achievements, Apple Health Mindful Minutes integration, Cloud Backup
- Privacy posture: data on device

**Implication 1: H2 is dead-by-redundancy.** Bingen already shipped the wedge.
**Implication 2: Efe's solo Blocker is in direct collision with Eclipta** — same lane, Efe's friend's already-shipped product. This is now a load-bearing decision for the call.
**Implication 3: H6 (Eclipta cofound) becomes much more concrete and the clear front-runner.** Not "join the meditation app" — "join the block+meditate combo that just launched 3 weeks ago and needs growth."

## The 4 options to bring to the call

Ranked by leverage. Don't pitch a single wedge — open with discovery, then layer the option that aligns with what Bingen tells you.

### Option A — Discover first, don't pitch (start here regardless)

This is what your original `ideas/cofounding-bingen.md` §First-call agenda already said. The validation results sharpen it.

> Don't pitch. Open with questions. If you try to solve your gap before seeing his, it stops being a partnership and becomes a monologue.

The opening sequence stays:
1. "What's been missing most in your life lately — a build partner, or something else?"
2. "How's Eclipta going — solo, team, what isn't working?" — **this question is now the load-bearing one.** His answer determines whether you go to Option B / C / D.
3. "If we built something together, what's the **own** problem you'd be solving for yourself?"

Then choose B / C / D based on what he says.

### Option B — H6: Efe joins Eclipta as cofounder (NOW THE CLEAR FRONT-RUNNER)

**What this is:** Bingen has already shipped the exact product the validation chain pointed to. Eclipta launched April 7, 2026 — three weeks ago — and is in the post-launch growth window. Instead of building NEW together (and going through another validation chain that just told you "5 of 7 hypotheses don't have markets"), you join him on Eclipta. He stays product owner / vision / inner-protocol IP. You bring UX, distribution, growth, and dev velocity.

**Why this is the clear front-runner now:**
- Skips the "find a market" phase entirely. Eclipta is shipped, paid pricing is live, real users are downloading.
- The validation chain effectively confirmed the wedge IS real (H2 had strong intensity on HN — block-bypass-rage cycle is a documented pain) — Bingen's wedge, not a hypothetical merge.
- Bingen-fit is automatic.
- Doesn't require Efe to abandon Unit (Eclipta = evenings/weekends cofound).
- The "would I want to grow Eclipta even if I weren't friends with Bingen?" test is answerable — and the answer is more honest than the original parallel-build wedge could ever be, because there's a real product to look at.

**What you bring (concretely, if Bingen invites it):**
- ASO + landing page + onboarding rework — first 30 seconds of Eclipta probably under-explains what makes it different. "Master your focus by bridging the gap" is abstract; users need a concrete first-session win in 60 seconds.
- Distribution-from-zero playbook — 0 ratings is a discoverability tax. You've been shipping and writing publicly for months; he hasn't (as far as you know — verify on the call).
- Pricing/packaging audit — ₺499/mo is high for a 3-week-old app with no social proof. Trial flow + price-test loop matters.
- A growth instrumentation loop that respects the "data on device" privacy stance. Hard but doable.
- Optional: Blocker IP rolled into Eclipta as an "extreme block" tier or hard-mode setting.

**Diagnostic questions to ask Bingen on the call (replaces the old "WTP signal hunting" frame):**
1. **Acquisition source** — where are Eclipta's first downloads coming from? Organic ASO? Your network? Paid? *(If <100 total installs and all from network, the growth wedge is "build distribution from zero" — multi-month. If >1000 organic in 3 weeks, the wedge is "amplify what's working.")*
2. **Activation rate** — % of installs that complete first meditation + first block. *(If <30%, onboarding is the pinned issue.)*
3. **D7 retention + paid conversion** — D7/D30 install→retain, free→paid. *(Anchor for unit economics. If D7 <20%, retention is the pinned issue.)*
4. **Are you open to a cofounder, or is this your solo thing?** *(This is the load-bearing variable. The hypothesis dies if he wants to keep Eclipta solo. Don't ask this in the first 10 minutes — earn it after the diagnostic data conversation.)*
5. **What's your vision for Eclipta's next 6 months?** — does he want to push into workout/lift territory, or stay in pure focus/meditation lane? *(Your growth ideas only matter if they align with his direction. If he says "I want to keep it pure meditation/focus, no body integration," your overlap with Unit/Blocker is much tighter and harder.)*

**The hard parts:**
- Equity asymmetry. Bingen shipped first; you'd be joining a started company. Standard cofounder vesting on contribution, not equal split. Don't bring this up on call #1; flag it for call #2.
- Blocker collision (see below — this is the most awkward thing on the agenda).
- Single-platform reach — Apple-only narrows TAM. If he's anti-Android by conviction, your distribution levers are bounded.

### The Blocker × Eclipta collision (handle on call #1 or #2)

You've been planning Blocker as your next solo product. **It is in the same lane as Eclipta.** This isn't a soft overlap — it's the same wedge.

Three honest paths, in order of clean-ness:

1. **Kill Blocker, cofound Eclipta.** Cleanest. You don't ship a product that competes with your closest friend's already-launched app. Time freed up = invest in Eclipta growth.
2. **Absorb Blocker IP into Eclipta as an "extreme block" mode or differentiated tier.** Bingen accepts you as cofounder + your IP folds in.
3. **Differentiate Blocker hard.** Not by feature (you'll lose) — by audience or platform: B2B-only, Android-only, school-network-only. Risky — you'd be competing in the same category as your cofounder candidate. The friendship-meets-business test gets ugly fast.

**Recommendation:** if Bingen invites you on Eclipta (Option B alive), pick path 1 or 2. If he wants to stay solo (Option B dead), Blocker stays your solo product but you should reframe it to NOT compete with Eclipta directly — find a niche he won't go into.

**This was previously H2. It's now a different thing entirely.**

If on the call Bingen says (a) Eclipta is consuming him solo and he doesn't want a cofounder there, AND (b) he wants to build something fresh with you anyway, the validation chain says the easy wedges are picked over. The lanes that survived (H1, H3, H4, H5, H7 are all dead) are mostly empty. This is the open-ended option. Lowest leverage, but honest if A and B both fail.

### Option D — Don't cofound a product. Cofound the protocol.

**Honor [[dostluk-yasatmak]]. Run the daily cowork+lift+meditate as a private 1:1 ritual without making it software.**

The original brief's decision gate already named this:
> "Would I build this product alone if Bingen weren't in the picture?"
> No → this isn't cofounding, it's wrapping the friendship in a project. Pull back.

The Eclipta correction sharpens this option, doesn't remove it. Even with Eclipta as a cofound option, **Option D is the right answer if the call reveals**:
- Bingen wants Eclipta solo (Option B dead).
- He doesn't have a clear next thing for you both.
- Either of you is in a season where adding a venture is the wrong move (Efe's Unit + Colonist + life flux in Aydın; Bingen's solo-Eclipta-grind).

**What Option D actually looks like:**
- Daily 60-min sync cowork via Zoom (Efe Aydın morning = Bingen Madrid morning).
- Weekly shared lift session over FaceTime, no software required.
- Monthly deep-talk call.
- Both keep solo products. Unit ships. Blocker ships (or pivots to not-Eclipta-collision). Eclipta grows.
- No equity, no IP, no ops. Zero coordination cost.

**Why this is a real option, not a failure:**
- Frees Efe to ship Unit + Blocker without splitting attention.
- Frees Bingen to grow Eclipta solo (and you remain a no-equity advisor / friend).
- The friendship is the asset, and it gets nurtured directly — not through a venture wrapper.
- If a real wedge surfaces in 6-12 months (Bingen's Eclipta plateaus, Efe's apps hit a different ceiling, the market shifts), the protocol-friendship is already in place. The cofounding can come later, naturally.

This is the pull-back option. **It is not a loss.** It's the read of "we're not at product yet, and that's fine."

## Recommended call structure

1. **Open with Option A.** Discovery questions. Listen for 20-30 minutes. Don't pitch anything.

2. **Branch on what he says:**
   - **Bingen lights up about Eclipta — talks about onboarding pain, can't find users, wants to push into workout territory** → Option B (Eclipta join). Run the diagnostic questions naturally. Don't ask "are you open to a cofounder?" in the first 15 min — earn it after you've shown you understand his data.
   - **Bingen is happy with Eclipta as solo + has a different itch he wants to scratch with you** → Option C (find a fresh problem together). Don't try to fit it into one of the dead hypotheses.
   - **Bingen sounds overwhelmed by Eclipta / mid-burnout / mid-other-life-thing** → Option D (protocol friendship). Don't make it a venture pitch. Frame it as "let's keep the daily protocol alive remotely, no app needed." This is also the right move if YOUR own situation (Aydın flux, Unit shipping, Colonist commitments) means you can't honestly commit time to Eclipta or anything else right now.

3. **Don't decide on call #1.** The decision gate Efe wrote (2 weeks out, "would I build this alone if Bingen weren't in the picture") still holds. Layer the new question: "Would I want to grow Eclipta if it weren't Bingen's app?"

4. **Equity, time-budget, location** — keep these for call #2. First call is shape, not structure.

5. **The Blocker × Eclipta collision** — surface it on call #1 if Option B is alive, even if briefly. Don't let it lurk into call #3 — that's where friendship-business friction starts.

## What to NOT bring to the call

- The original parallel-build wedge as a leading pitch. The validation killed it. If you lead with it, you're selling Bingen on a product that has no market evidence — that's a bad start to a partnership.
- A "let's build the block+meditate combo together" pitch. He already shipped it. If you describe the H2 wedge as if it's an opportunity, he'll either be confused or feel you didn't read his app.
- A "let's just build something fast" framing. Both of you ship products solo today. Adding a third without validation is how cofounded ventures die in month 4.
- Anything about equity on call #1.
- Any whiff of "I need this venture for my Aydın loneliness" — that's a [[yalnizlik]] problem, not a cofounding shape, and Bingen will smell it.
