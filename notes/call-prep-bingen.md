# Bingen call prep

## Bottom line

Do not pitch the old parallel-build idea. The validation killed it.

Do not pitch "Blocker + meditation" as a new idea. Bingen already shipped that shape with Eclipta.

The real question for the call:

> Should Efe join Eclipta, find a different problem with Bingen, or keep this as a friendship protocol instead of a company?

## What changed

- The remote build/lift/meditate product did not have market evidence.
- Eclipta is not just meditation. It is already "screen time + focus" with app blocking until meditation.
- That makes Efe's Blocker plan directly overlap with Eclipta.
- The only strong startup path right now is Efe joining Eclipta, if Bingen wants that.

## Call goal

Leave with one of these outcomes:

1. **Eclipta path** - Bingen is open to help or a cofounder conversation.
2. **Fresh-problem path** - Bingen wants to build with Efe, but not on Eclipta.
3. **Protocol path** - no startup yet; keep the daily cowork/lift/deep-talk rhythm alive.

Do not decide equity, title, or ownership on this call.

## Opening questions

Ask these first. Listen before suggesting anything.

1. "How is Eclipta actually going?"
2. "What is the hardest part right now: product, users, retention, pricing, or energy?"
3. "Are you building it as a solo thing, or have you thought about a team?"
4. "If we built something together, what problem would you personally want solved?"
5. "What would make a cofounding setup exciting rather than heavy?"

## If Eclipta is alive

Use this branch if Bingen talks about Eclipta traction, growth problems, onboarding, retention, or wanting help.

Ask:

- How many installs so far?
- Where are users coming from?
- What percent complete first meditation plus first block?
- What is D7 retention?
- Any paid conversions yet?
- What feedback have users repeated?
- What do you want Eclipta to become in 6 months?

Possible Efe contribution:

- Sharpen onboarding and first-session win.
- Improve App Store positioning and screenshots.
- Build a growth loop around focus pain, not generic meditation.
- Test pricing and trial flow.
- Fold Blocker ideas into Eclipta instead of competing with it.

Decision after this branch:

> Would I want to grow Eclipta even if it were not Bingen's app?

If yes, schedule call #2 for scope, time budget, and equity.

## If Bingen wants something new

Use this branch if he wants to build together but not inside Eclipta.

Say:

> The ideas I came in with did not survive validation. If we build something new, we should start from a real complaint, not from the Milan story.

Then map:

- What he is currently angry about.
- What users he already understands.
- What he would work on for a year.
- Where Efe has unfair speed or distribution.

Do not force a product idea in this call.

## If no startup is right now

Use this branch if Bingen wants Eclipta solo, feels overloaded, or neither of you has real time.

Propose the protocol:

- One daily cowork block.
- One weekly lift or training sync.
- One monthly deep-talk call.
- No equity.
- No shared product.
- Revisit in 2-3 months if a real wedge appears.

This protects the friendship without pretending it is a company.

## Blocker decision

This has to be handled directly if Eclipta becomes serious.

Clean options:

1. Kill Blocker and help grow Eclipta.
2. Fold Blocker IP into Eclipta.
3. Differentiate Blocker hard by market or platform.

Best default:

> If Bingen wants Efe on Eclipta, choose option 1 or 2. Do not build a near-competitor in parallel.

## Do not bring

- The old parallel-build pitch.
- A generic "let's build something fast" pitch.
- Equity on call #1.
- Loneliness as the hidden reason for starting a company.
- A long validation recap unless Bingen asks.

## Pre-call homework

- Use Eclipta for 5 days.
- Write down 3 friction points and 3 things that work.
- Compare Eclipta honestly against Blocker.
- Decide whether Efe is actually excited to grow Eclipta.
- Keep the call exploratory, not performative.
