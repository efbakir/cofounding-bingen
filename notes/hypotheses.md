# Hypotheses — active board

`idea-explorer` agent'ı buraya append eder. Manuel ekleme de OK — sadece formatı koru.

Status değerleri: `fresh` (henüz hunt edilmedi) → `hunting` (problem-hunter çalışıyor) → `validated` (pain-validator GO verdi) → `mapping` (community-mapper çalışıyor) → `posting` (build-in-public aşamasında) → `live` (tester funnel çalışıyor) → `paused` / `killed`

---

## Format örneği (sil bunu, gerçek H1 ekleyince)

## H1 — {one-line problem statement} — YYYY-MM-DD

**Who specifically** — {persona, role, company size, geography}
**What they do today** — {current workaround, tool, or workaround pain}
**Why it sucks** — {the friction, in one sentence}
**WTP signal to look for** — {what kind of phrase in Reddit/HN would prove they'd pay}
**Where to hunt** — {2-4 specific subs, forums, or communities to check first}
**Kill criteria** — {what evidence would make you drop this hypothesis in 1 week}
**Status** — fresh
